Channel Uploader

Channel Uploader is a private, single-user command-line tool. The channel owner runs it on their own PC to upload finished short videos to their own YouTube channels, schedule when each video goes public, and attach captions and translated titles.

It is not offered to other users and has no public sign-up.

채널 주인이 자기 PC에서 자기 YouTube 채널에 완성된 쇼츠를 올리고 공개 시각을 예약하는 개인용 도구입니다. 다른 사용자에게 제공하지 않습니다.
